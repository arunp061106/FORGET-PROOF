Compliance Score Formula & Components

Label: Our own summary metric, not a regulatory standard.

The compliance score evaluates how effectively the system fulfills right-to-be-forgotten requests across four core pillars (total: 100 points):

  • Fulfillment Rate (30 pts max): Percentage of submitted withdrawal requests successfully executed without errors. Earned: 30.0 / 30.0.
  • Speed & Latency (25 pts max): Wall-clock retrain latency benchmarked against the monolithic retrain standard. Earned: 24.1 / 25.0.
  • Privacy Protection (25 pts max): Membership inference mitigation: 0.0 pts awarded. Leakage reduction is -0.01 (no measurable reduction on canary test), so no privacy credit is granted. Earned: 0.0 / 25.0.
  • Chain Integrity (20 pts max): Cryptographic verification of the SHA-256 hash chain and HMAC-SHA256 signatures. Earned: 20.0 / 20.0.

Total Recomputed Score: 74.1 / 100 (Grade B).

Note: This internal scoring framework is designed to help operators benchmark unlearning service quality, but does not constitute legal or regulatory certification under GDPR, HIPAA, or CCPA.
